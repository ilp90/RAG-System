Make informed choices with detailed nutritional information from the Locations page. Some descriptions are also available on menu screens and labels in each location. Plus, our knowledgeable staff is always happy to answer any specific questions you may have.
How to Check Ingredient Labels in Dining Centers
- Locate ingredient labels next to dishes you’re interested in.
- Reference allergens and suitable diets listed on the label.
- Use your phone’s camera to scan the QR code for more.
- Once you’ve scanned the code, reference ingredients.
- If information is missing, resize your browser in settings.
