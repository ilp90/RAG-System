Composting
The Bell Pepper is one example of hundreds of ingredients we strategically use. Putting unused portions of the pepper (pre-consumer) and the post-consumer food waste into compost means that the waste gets new life by biodegrading into nutrient rich soil. These efforts help us be more sustainable, and lower food cost, meaning we can be good stewards of students' dollars.
